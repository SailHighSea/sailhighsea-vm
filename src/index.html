A lightweight voice meter and volume mixer: per-app volume, multi-device output, equalizer, compressor and gate.

Set the volume of every app and output in one place, and move an app to another output by dragging it. Click extra outputs to mirror an app to several devices at once, with a delay trim to line them up.

Shape an app's sound with the 10-band equalizer, compressor and noise gate. Closing the window keeps the mixer running in the tray; click the tray icon for the quick popup.

Settings and logs are kept next to the program.
