Delivery notes

  • Windows: ship the mono glyph as white and black PNG/ICO sets (16, 20, 24, 32 px at 100–200 % scaling) and pick one by reading AppsUseLightTheme from the registry, switching live when it changes.
  • macOS: ship one black-with-alpha PNG and mark it as a template image (Tauri: icon_as_template(true)) so the menu bar tints it for light, dark and highlighted states.
  • Linux: use the same black/white pair; many panels (GNOME, KDE) prefer a symbolic icon named …-symbolic.
  • Rules for the glyph: one colour, no gradients, no outer tile, strokes at least 1.5 px at 16 px, 1 px empty margin. Opacity steps (60 %) are fine because they stay one-colour.
  • States: normal, muted (slash), and an optional small dot for "equalizer or mirror active". The full-colour icon is only used for the executable, installer and taskbar.
